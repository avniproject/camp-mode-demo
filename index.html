Camp ModeAvni 18.3 · three phones, no internet

A mobile clinic sets up in a village with no network. The registration desk, the nurse and the doctor each work on their own phone, and the same patient moves between them as one team.

Step 1 of 12

One phone is the hub. It starts its own Wi-Fi, and the other phones join it by scanning its code.
Joined phones share every 30 seconds by themselves. Nobody presses a button.
Nothing goes to the server during the camp. When the camp ends and the internet is back, each phone uploads its own records.
Everyone and everything in this demo is made up.
